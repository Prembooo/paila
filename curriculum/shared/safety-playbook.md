# Safety Playbook (सुरक्षा निर्देशिका)

Reusable safety patterns referenced by ID from lessons, so the same trusted steps appear everywhere. Tone is safety-first and never fear-based: every risk is paired with a calm, constructive action (Requirements 6.7, 11.5, 11.6, 15.1).

## SP-1 — Suspected scam or unsafe situation (3 steps, in order)
1. **Stop and do not respond.** रोक्नुहोस्, जवाफ नदिनुहोस्।
2. **Do not share** any personal information or money. कुनै व्यक्तिगत जानकारी वा पैसा नबाँड्नुहोस्।
3. **Consult a trusted person** (family member, teacher, or facilitator) before doing anything else. केही गर्नुअघि भरपर्दो व्यक्ति (परिवार, शिक्षक वा सहजकर्ता) सँग सल्लाह गर्नुहोस्।

_Use in: D4 scam lessons, and anywhere a risk is shown._

## SP-2 — Unexpected money or credential request (refuse + verify)
- **Refuse** the request. अनुरोध अस्वीकार गर्नुहोस्।
- **Verify** through a *trusted channel*: contact the known person or official provider using contact details **you already have**, never the details given in the message. भरपर्दो माध्यमबाट जाँच्नुहोस् — आफूसँग पहिल्यैको सम्पर्क प्रयोग गर्नुहोस्, सन्देशमा दिइएको होइन।
- Never share a **PIN, OTP, or password**. PIN, OTP वा पासवर्ड कहिल्यै नबाँड्नुहोस्।
- **If you cannot verify:** take no action, share nothing, and ask a trusted person for help. जाँच्न नसके केही नगर्नुहोस्, केही नबाँड्नुहोस्, भरपर्दो व्यक्तिसँग मद्दत माग्नुहोस्।

_Use in: D4-M5 Digital Money Safety._

## SP-3 — Verify before you rely (information & AI)
- Cross-check an important claim against **at least 2 independent sources** (not all from the same author/publisher). महत्त्वपूर्ण दाबीलाई कम्तीमा २ स्वतन्त्र स्रोतसँग मिलाउनुहोस्।
- AI can be confidently wrong (an *AI hallucination*). Treat its answer as a starting point, then verify. AI गलत हुन सक्छ; जवाफलाई सुरुवात मात्र मानेर जाँच्नुहोस्।
- **If a claim cannot be confirmed or sources conflict:** treat it as unverified, do not share it as true, and consult a trusted person. पुष्टि नभए वा स्रोत बाझिए — साँचो भनेर नबाँड्नुहोस्, भरपर्दो व्यक्तिसँग सल्लाह गर्नुहोस्।

_Use in: D3 lessons and every Guided AI Exploration verify step._

## SP-4 — Feeling upset or distressed online
- It is okay to **pause**. रोक्न ठीकै हो।
- Step away from the screen and **talk to a trusted adult or facilitator**. पर्दाबाट हट्नुहोस् र भरपर्दो वयस्क वा सहजकर्तासँग कुरा गर्नुहोस्।

_Use in: D5 attention/emotion lessons (distress prompt)._

## SP-5 — Shared / family device hygiene
- Do not save passwords or personal logins on a shared device. साझा उपकरणमा पासवर्ड/लगइन नबचत गर्नुहोस्।
- Before passing the device on, **clear any personal data** you entered. अर्कोलाई दिनुअघि आफूले हालेको व्यक्तिगत जानकारी मेटाउनुहोस्।

_Use in: any Practical Activity on lab/family devices._
