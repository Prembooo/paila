# Project Themes (परियोजना विषयहरू)

Learners choose a theme for their Mini Projects and Capstone. Themes are shown in Nepali before a project begins. A learner may select one, change it without losing progress, or be given the default if none is chosen. Off-list themes are allowed only if they contain no personal/sensitive information and are safety-conformant (Requirement 17).

Schema: `id` · `name (EN)` · `नाम (NE)` · `is_default`.

| ID | Name (EN) | नाम (NE) | Default? | Example project idea |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TH-01 | Football | फुटबल | ✓ (default) | Find and verify a fact about a favorite player or local tournament |
| TH-02 | Art & music | कला र संगीत | — | Explain a local art form using verified sources |
| TH-03 | Farming & agriculture | खेती र कृषि | — | Research a better way to grow a local crop, checked against 2 sources |
| TH-04 | Local festivals & culture | स्थानीय चाडपर्व र संस्कृति | — | A short explainer on the history of a local festival |
| TH-05 | Local marketplace & shops | स्थानीय बजार र पसल | — | Compare prices/info and spot one misleading claim |
| TH-06 | Health & wellbeing | स्वास्थ्य र तन्दुरुस्ती | — | Verify a common health belief against trustworthy sources |
| TH-07 | Environment & nature | वातावरण र प्रकृति | — | Explain a local environment issue with checked facts |

## Rules
- **Default:** if a learner does not choose, assign **TH-01 Football** so the project can proceed (Req 17.3).
- **Change:** a learner may switch themes before finishing without losing prior progress (Req 17.4).
- **Off-list:** allow a learner-proposed theme only if it has no personal/sensitive info and follows the safety-first principle; otherwise redirect to this list (Req 17.5).
