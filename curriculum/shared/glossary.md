# Glossary (शब्दकोश)

Every technical term is defined here in simple English with a Nepali equivalent, and must be defined before its first use in a lesson (Requirements 13.7, 13.8). Keep definitions short and everyday.

Schema: `term` · `definition (simple English)` · `परिभाषा (नेपाली)` · `first used in`.

| Term | Simple definition (EN) | परिभाषा (NE) | First used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Device | A machine like a phone or computer you can use | फोन वा कम्प्युटर जस्तो प्रयोग गर्न मिल्ने यन्त्र | D1-M1 |
| Hardware | The parts of a device you can touch | छुन मिल्ने उपकरणका भागहरू | D1-M2 |
| Software | The programs and apps that run on a device | उपकरणमा चल्ने एप र प्रोग्रामहरू | D1-M2 |
| Operating system | The main program that lets you control a device and open apps | उपकरण चलाउने र एप खोल्ने मुख्य प्रोग्राम | D1-M2 |
| App | A program you open to do one kind of task | एउटा काम गर्न खोल्ने प्रोग्राम | D1-M1 |
| Internet | The network that connects devices to share information | जानकारी साटासाट गर्न उपकरण जोड्ने सञ्जाल | D2-M1 |
| Wi-Fi | One way a device connects to the internet without wires | तारविना इन्टरनेटमा जोडिने एउटा तरिका | D2-M1 |
| Browser | An app used to open websites | वेबसाइट खोल्ने एप | D2-M1 |
| Website | A place on the internet with pages of information | इन्टरनेटमा जानकारीका पृष्ठ भएको ठाउँ | D2-M1 |
| Search engine | A tool that helps you find things on the internet | इन्टरनेटमा कुरा खोज्न मद्दत गर्ने उपकरण | D2-M2 |
| AI (artificial intelligence) | Software that can answer questions and make guesses like a helper | प्रश्नको जवाफ दिने र अनुमान गर्ने सहायक सफ्टवेयर | D2-M3 |
| AI hallucination | When AI gives a confident answer that is actually wrong | AI ले विश्वासका साथ दिएको तर गलत जवाफ | D3-M4 |
| Algorithm | The hidden rules an app uses to choose what to show you | एपले के देखाउने भनेर छान्ने लुकेका नियम | D2-M3 |
| Misinformation | Information that is false or misleading | झूटो वा भ्रम पार्ने जानकारी | D3-M3 |
| Source | Where a piece of information came from | जानकारी आएको ठाउँ वा व्यक्ति | D3-M2 |
| Digital footprint | The trail of information you leave online | अनलाइनमा छाडिने जानकारीको डोब | D4-M1 |
| Password | A secret word or code that protects your account | खाता जोगाउने गोप्य शब्द वा कोड | D4-M3 |
| Two-step verification | A second check, like a code, to protect your account | खाता जोगाउन दोस्रो जाँच, जस्तै कोड | D4-M3 |
| Scam | A trick used to steal money or information | पैसा वा जानकारी चोर्ने धोका | D4-M4 |
| Synthetic media / deepfake | Fake images, voice, or video made by AI | AI ले बनाएको नक्कली फोटो, आवाज वा भिडियो | D4-M4 |
| OTP | A one-time code sent to confirm something; never share it | पुष्टि गर्न आउने एक-पटके कोड; कहिल्यै नबाँड्ने | D4-M4 |
| Mobile wallet | A phone app used to send or receive money | पैसा पठाउने/लिने मोबाइल एप | D4-M5 |
| Attention economy | How apps are built to keep you using them longer | एपले बढी समय प्रयोग गराउन बनाइने तरिका | D5-M2 |
| Digital identity | How you appear to others online | अनलाइनमा अरूले देख्ने तपाईंको पहिचान | D5-M5 |
