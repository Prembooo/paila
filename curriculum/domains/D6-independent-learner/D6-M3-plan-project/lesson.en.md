# Plan Your Project

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can plan a small learning project.
You can break a goal into easy steps.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

A project is a goal you work towards over time.
A good plan makes a big goal feel easy.

The trick is to break it into small steps.
Each small step is something you can finish today.

Start with your goal in one clear sentence.
Then list three or four steps to reach it.

For each step, note what you need.
You may need the internet, a book, or a person to ask.

A plan can change as you learn — that is fine.
The plan guides you; it does not trap you.

![Break a goal into a few small steps you can finish.](figures/project-steps.svg)

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| Part of a plan | Example |
|---|---|
| Goal | "Learn to grow tomatoes at home." |
| Step 1 | Find out what seeds and soil I need. |
| Step 2 | Watch two short how-to videos. |
| Step 3 | Plant seeds and water them daily. |
| Step 4 | Note what works and ask for help. |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. Write your project goal in one sentence.
2. List three small steps to reach it.
3. For one step, note what you need.
4. Say which step you will do first.

**Success:** you wrote a goal and three clear steps.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Write a small project plan for your topic: one goal and three steps.
For example: "Goal: learn a new song. Steps: find the words, practise the tune, sing it for family."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
