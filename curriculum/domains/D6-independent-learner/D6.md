# Domain 6 — Becoming an Independent Learner (Capstone)
# डोमेन ६ — स्वतन्त्र सिकारु बन्ने (क्यापस्टोन)

> Phase 1 · Sequence 6 · Outcome **O12** · 4 lessons · Capstone domain.

---

## English overview

Domain 6 is the capstone. Here learners bring together every skill from
Domains 1 to 5. They learn how to learn anything on their own. They ask
better questions, find trustworthy resources, and plan a small project.
At the end, each learner makes one capstone artifact.

The tone is safety-first and never fear-based. Every risk is paired with
a calm, helpful action. New terms are defined in simple words, with
Nepali, before use (see `shared/glossary.md`).

Learners build one outcome:

- **O12** — Plan and complete a self-directed learning project. The
  learner produces a cited, verified capstone artifact.

These skills connect to the safety playbook **SP-3 (verify before you
rely)**, which is reinforced in the AI-literacy lessons (in Check Your
Understanding and the Activity). Learners choose a project theme from
`shared/project-themes.md` (default Football).

The capstone in D6-M4 must show at least one observable skill from each
of the six domains (D1–D6). It must cite at least two sources and
describe the verify steps. A privacy check is done before sharing.

## नेपाली सार (Nepali overview)

डोमेन ६ क्यापस्टोन हो। यहाँ विद्यार्थीले डोमेन १ देखि ५ सम्मका सबै सीप
सँगै ल्याउँछन्। आफैं जे पनि सिक्ने तरिका सिक्छन्। राम्रो प्रश्न सोध्छन्,
भरपर्दो स्रोत खोज्छन्, र सानो परियोजना योजना गर्छन्। अन्त्यमा हरेक
विद्यार्थीले एउटा क्यापस्टोन रचना बनाउँछन्।

शैली सुरक्षा-पहिलो छ, कहिल्यै डर देखाउने होइन। हरेक जोखिमसँग एउटा सरल,
सहयोगी काम जोडिएको छ। नयाँ शब्द सजिलो भाषामा, नेपालीसहित, प्रयोगअघि
बुझाइन्छ (`shared/glossary.md` हेर्नुहोस्)।

विद्यार्थीले एउटा परिणाम बनाउँछन्:

- **O12** — आफ्नै सिकाइ परियोजना योजना गरी पूरा गर्ने। विद्यार्थीले
  स्रोत उद्धृत गरी, जाँचिएको क्यापस्टोन रचना बनाउँछन्।

यी सीप सुरक्षा निर्देशिका **SP-3 (भरोसाअघि जाँच्नुहोस्)** सँग जोडिन्छन्।
यो AI साक्षरता पाठहरूमा (आफ्नो बुझाइ जाँच र क्रियाकलापमा) दोहोरिन्छ। विद्यार्थीले
`shared/project-themes.md` बाट परियोजना विषय छान्छन् (पूर्वनिर्धारित फुटबल)।
