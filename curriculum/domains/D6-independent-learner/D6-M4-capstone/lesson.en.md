# Capstone Project

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can complete a small project on your own.
You will use the skills from all the chapters.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

This is your final project for Phase 1.
You will learn one new thing and show what you did.

You already have everything you need.
You can use a computer, search, judge sources, and stay safe.

Bring your skills together in four steps.
**Plan, learn, make, and share.**

First, plan: write your goal and three steps.
Then learn: search, check sources, and ask good questions.

Next, make: try the skill and create something small.
Finally, share: show your work and tell what you learned.

You can use AI to help along the way.
**AI literacy:** check its answers, and keep your work your own.
Stay safe too: do not share private information online.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| Step | What you do | Chapter it uses |
|---|---|---|
| Plan | Write a goal and steps | Independent learner |
| Learn | Search and check sources | Internet, information literacy |
| Make | Try the skill, create something | Devices, independent learner |
| Share | Show your work safely | Safety, social media |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near, over more than one session.

1. Plan your project: goal and three steps.
2. Learn: find two trustworthy sources; note where each came from and how you checked them.
3. Make: write a short one-page explainer (or draw a poster) on your topic.
4. Share: check there is no private information, then tell the class one thing you learned.

**Success:** you planned, learned, made, and shared a small project.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Do a small capstone project on your topic using plan, learn, make, and share.
For example: "I learned a passing drill, made a one-page poster, and listed my two sources."
There is no single right answer — your project is what matters.
