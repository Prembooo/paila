# Parts of a Computer

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

This lesson serves **O01**.
By the end, you can **name the main parts of a computer** and say what each one does.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

A computer is a machine that helps us do many tasks.
We type, watch, learn, and make things with it.
A computer is made of a few main parts.
Each part has its own simple job.
Let us look at them together in the picture below.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

Look at each number in the picture. Here is the name and the job of each part.

| # | Part | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Monitor (screen)** | Shows what the computer is doing, like a TV. |
| 2 | **System unit (the box)** | The "brain box". It holds the CPU that thinks and works. |
| 3 | **Keyboard** | You press keys to type letters and numbers. |
| 4 | **Mouse** | You move it to point and click on the screen. |
| 5 | **Camera (webcam)** | Lets the computer see you for a video call. |
| 6 | **Storage** | Keeps your files saved, even after you turn it off. |

> **Two helper words:**
> *CPU* — the part inside the box that thinks and does the work (कम्प्युटरको दिमाग).
> *Storage* — the part that remembers and saves your files (फाइल बचत गर्ने भाग).

A laptop has these same parts joined into one folding piece.
A smartphone also has a screen, a brain, and storage — just very small.

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this on a lab computer, with your facilitator near.

1. Point to the **monitor**. Say its name aloud.
2. Point to the **keyboard** and the **mouse**. Say what each does.
3. Move the mouse and watch the arrow move on the screen.
4. Press one letter key and see it appear on the screen.

**Success:** you pointed to 4 parts and said the job of each.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Draw or describe how you would use a computer for it.
Name **two parts** you would use, and why.
For example: "I use the **monitor** to watch a match and the **mouse** to choose the video."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
