# First Hands-On: Using a Computer

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

This lesson serves **O01**.
By the end, you can **turn on a computer, open an app, and turn it off safely**.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

Now we use a real computer. Take your time. It is okay to go slow.
You already know the parts. Here is the order we use them.

1. **Turn on:** press the power button on the system unit.
2. **Wait:** the operating system starts and shows the home screen.
3. **Use the mouse:** move it to point; press the left button to **click**.
4. **Open an app:** click an app icon once or twice.
5. **Type:** use the keyboard to write letters.
6. **Turn off:** use the screen's shut-down button, not the wall switch.

![A desktop computer and its parts: screen, system unit, keyboard, mouse](figures/computer-parts.svg)

## 3. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this on a lab computer, with your facilitator near.

1. Press the **power button** and wait for the home screen.
2. Move the **mouse** and watch the arrow.
3. **Open** one app by clicking its icon.
4. **Type** your first name with the keyboard.
5. **Close** the app.
6. **Turn off** the computer the safe way.

**Success:** you turned it on, opened an app, typed, and turned it off safely.

**If it is hard:** that is normal. Ask your facilitator to show you once. Then try again. You do not lose your progress.

## 5. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Turn on the computer, open one app, and type something about your topic.
For example: open an app and type your favourite team's name. Then turn off safely.
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
