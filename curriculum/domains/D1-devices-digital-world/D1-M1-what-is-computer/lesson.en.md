# What Is a Computer?

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

This lesson serves **O01**.
By the end, you can say **what a computer is** and name **four kinds** of computer.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

A computer is a machine that helps us do work.
A computer can do three big things.
It can **think** (work out answers).
It can **show** (put things on a screen).
It can **store** (keep your files).

Computers come in different sizes.
A big one sits on a table. A small one fits in your hand.
But they all think, show, and store.
Let us look at four kinds in the picture.

![Four kinds of computer: desktop, laptop, tablet, and smartphone, numbered 1 to 4](figures/devices.svg)

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| # | Kind | Where you see it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Desktop** | Sits on a table; used in offices and labs. |
| 2 | **Laptop** | Folds shut; you can carry it. |
| 3 | **Tablet** | A flat screen you touch with a finger. |
| 4 | **Smartphone** | A small computer that also makes calls. |

In our lab, we mostly learn on the **desktop** computer.
It is the best one to learn the main parts and skills.

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this in the lab or your home, with your facilitator.

1. Point to the **desktop** computer in the lab.
2. Name one other kind of computer you have seen.
3. Say one thing a computer can do: think, show, or store.

**Success:** you named the desktop and one more kind of computer.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a singer, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Say which kind of computer you would use to learn about it, and why.
For example: "To follow football, I would use a desktop in the lab to watch matches."
There is no single right answer — your choice and your reason are what matter.
