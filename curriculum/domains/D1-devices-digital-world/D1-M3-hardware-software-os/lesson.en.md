# Hardware, Software & the Operating System

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

This lesson serves **O01**.
By the end, you can explain **hardware**, **software**, and the **operating system** in simple words.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

A computer has two sides that work together.

**Hardware** is the parts you can **touch**.
The screen, keyboard, and mouse are hardware.

**Software** is the **programs** you cannot touch.
A game, a video app, or a learning app is software.

One special software is the boss: the **operating system**.
*Operating system* — the main program that lets you control the device and open apps.
When you turn on a computer, the operating system starts first.
Then it lets you open your apps.

Look at the picture. You use apps on top. Apps sit on the system. The system runs the hardware.

![Three layers: hardware at the bottom, operating system in the middle, apps on top, with a person using the apps](figures/os-layers.svg)

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| Word | What it is | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Hardware** | parts you can touch | screen, keyboard, mouse, CPU |
| **Software** | programs you cannot touch | a game, a video app, a learning app |
| **Operating system** | the boss program that runs everything | the home screen with all your app icons |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this on a lab computer, with your facilitator.

1. Touch one piece of **hardware** and say its name.
2. Point to one **software** app on the screen.
3. Open that app, then close it again.

**Success:** you named one hardware part and opened one software app.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Name one **hardware** part and one **software** app you would use for it.
For example: "I touch the **keyboard** (hardware) and open a **video app** (software) to watch a match."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
