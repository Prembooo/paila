# Taking Back Your Time

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can name simple ways to control screen time.
You can also calm strong feelings that posts can cause.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

Scrolling for a long time can hurt your sleep and mood.
It can also pull your attention away from schoolwork.

The problem is not the number of minutes.
The real sign is when it feels hard to stop.

Posts can also stir strong feelings.
You may feel sad, angry, or "less than" others online.

Remember: people post their best moments, not their full lives.
What you see is a small, chosen part.

Here are calm habits that give you control:
set a time limit, turn off autoplay, and silence notifications.
Take real breaks and do offline things you enjoy.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| Feeling or habit | Calm action |
|---|---|
| Hard to stop scrolling | Set a timer; close the app when it rings. |
| Videos pull you on | Turn off autoplay in settings. |
| Phone keeps buzzing | Turn off notifications you do not need. |
| Feeling "less than" others | Remember posts show only the best parts. |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. Name one sign that screen time is too much.
2. Choose one calm habit from the table.
3. On a lab computer, find where to turn off autoplay or notifications.
4. Name one offline activity you enjoy.

**Success:** you chose one calm habit and one offline activity.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Make a small plan for your screen time when you enjoy your topic online.
Write one limit you will set, and one offline thing you will do.
For example: "I watch match clips for 20 minutes, then I go play outside."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
