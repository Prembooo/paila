# Why Social Media Exists

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can say what **social media** is.
You can name why these apps want your time.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

**Social media** are apps where people share and chat.
Examples are Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

These apps help us in good ways.
We talk to family, learn new things, and share fun.

But these apps are also a business.
They earn money by showing you **ads**.

The longer you stay, the more ads you see.
So the apps are built to keep you watching.

This is not bad to know — it helps you stay in control.
When you know the goal, you can choose your own time.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| The app gives you | The app wants |
|---|---|
| Chat with friends and family | Your attention and time |
| Fun videos and posts | To show you more ads |
| New things to learn | You to come back often |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. Name one social media app you know.
2. Say one good thing you do on it.
3. Say how the app might earn money from you.
4. Name one way you stay in control of your time.

**Success:** you named a good use and how the app earns money.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Imagine a social media post about your topic.
Write one good use of the app, and one way it tries to keep you longer.
For example: "Good: I watch match highlights. It autoplays the next video to keep me."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
