# The Attention Trap

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can explain how apps keep you scrolling.
You can name the tricks that make it hard to stop.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

Short videos play one after another, on their own.
This is on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

This is called **autoplay**: the next video starts by itself.
There is no natural place to stop, so you keep watching.

![Videos autoplay in an endless loop to keep you scrolling.](figures/attention-trap.svg)

The app studies what you like and shows more of it.
Each new video is a small surprise that pulls you on.

Soon, a few minutes can turn into a few hours.
This is not your fault — the app is built this way.

The good news: a few simple changes give the control back to you.
You will practise some of them in the next lesson.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| Trick the app uses | What it does to you |
|---|---|
| Autoplay | Starts the next video before you choose. |
| Endless scroll | The feed never ends, so you keep going. |
| Notifications | Pull you back into the app again and again. |
| "Just for you" videos | Show more of what keeps you watching. |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. On a lab computer, open a video app and find the autoplay setting.
2. Say one trick that makes it hard to stop.
3. Think of a time you watched longer than planned.
4. Name one small change to take back control.

**Success:** you named one trick and one change you can make.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Imagine you open an app to watch one video about your topic.
Write what the app does to keep you watching, and one way you stop.
For example: "It autoplays more match clips. I close the app after one video."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
