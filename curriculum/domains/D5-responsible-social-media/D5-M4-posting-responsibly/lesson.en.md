# Posting Responsibly

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can post and comment with kindness.
You can use one simple check before you share.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

When you post, other people read your words.
Your words can make someone feel good or hurt.

Online, we sometimes forget a real person is reading.
So it helps to pause before you post.

Use this simple check: **Is it true? Is it kind? Is it needed?**
If a post fails the check, it is better not to share.

Be careful about other people too.
Ask before you post a photo of a friend or family.

If someone is unkind to you online, you do not have to reply.
You can block, ignore, and tell a trusted adult.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| Before you post, ask | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Is it true? | Sharing false things can spread harm. |
| Is it kind? | Words can hurt a real person. |
| Is it needed? | Some things are better kept private. |
| Did I get permission? | Others may not want to be posted. |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. Read a sample post your facilitator shares.
2. Run the check: true, kind, needed?
3. Say if you would post it, and why.
4. Rewrite one unkind post in a kind way.

**Success:** you used the check and rewrote a post kindly.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Write one kind post about your topic that passes the check.
For example: "Great match today! Well played by both teams."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
