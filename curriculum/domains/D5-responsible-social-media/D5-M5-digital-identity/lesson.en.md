# Your Positive Digital Identity

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can describe your **digital identity**.
You can name ways to make it positive and true.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

Your **digital identity** is how you appear online.
It is built from your posts, photos, and comments over time.

Think of it like a garden you tend each day.
Kind, true, and helpful posts make a good garden.

A positive identity can help you later.
Teachers, groups, and jobs may see your online self.

You can share your real interests and skills.
For example: a drawing, a song, or a school project.

AI can help you here, used wisely.
You can ask an AI to improve the words of a post or bio.

**AI literacy:** AI gives a draft, not the final truth.
Always check it, and keep it true to who you are.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| Builds a positive identity | Better to avoid |
|---|---|
| Sharing your real skills | Pretending to be someone else |
| Kind, helpful comments | Unkind or angry posts |
| True information | Spreading rumours |
| A bio that sounds like you | Copying someone word for word |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. Name one skill or interest you could share.
2. Write a short, true bio line about yourself.
3. If you used AI, say what you would check.
4. Say one thing you would avoid posting.

**Success:** you wrote a true bio line and named one thing to avoid.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Write a short, true bio that shows your interest in your topic.
For example: "I love football and I help coach younger kids in my village."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
