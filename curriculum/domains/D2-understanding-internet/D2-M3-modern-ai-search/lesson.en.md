# Modern AI Search

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

This lesson serves **O03**.
By the end, you can **tell AI search from keyword search**.
You can also explain why you must verify answers.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

*AI* is software that answers questions like a helper.
A *keyword search* finds pages using the exact words you type.
Today many tools use AI to search too.
They work in different ways, so let us compare.

Here is the first difference: the result type.
Keyword search gives a list of links to open.
AI search writes one answer in full sentences.

Here is the second difference: how they read you.
Keyword search matches your exact words.
AI search guesses what you mean, even with loose words.

AI feels easy, but be careful.
AI can be confidently wrong, called an *AI hallucination*.
So treat its answer as a start, not the final truth.
Verify an important claim against at least two sources (SP-3).

Now learn how apps choose what you see.
An *algorithm* is the hidden rule an app uses.
It picks what to show you next.
It looks at what you watched before.
It also looks at what people like you watched.

This has a result you should notice.
You start to see more of the same things.
You may miss other useful things outside that circle.
It is like a shop showing only the brand you bought last time.

![You see more of the same; other useful things stay outside — a filter bubble.](figures/filter-bubble.svg)

You can act to stay open.
First action: search widely on purpose, not just easy clicks.
Second action: verify a claim against two sources before you trust it.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

Look at each number in the picture. Here is each kind of search.

| # | Kind of search | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Keyword search** | A list of links for you to open and read. |
| 2 | **AI search** | One written answer. It can still be wrong. |

> **Two helper words:**
> *AI hallucination* — when AI gives a confident answer that is wrong (विश्वासका साथ दिएको गलत जवाफ).
> *Algorithm* — the hidden rules an app uses to choose what to show you (के देखाउने छान्ने लुकेका नियम).

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this on a lab computer, with your facilitator near.

1. Search **best time to plant maize** with a keyword search.
2. Look at the list of links you get.
3. Now ask the same in an AI search.
4. Compare: a list of links, or one written answer?
5. Pick the answer's main claim and check two sources (SP-3).

**Success:** you compared both searches and verified one claim.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Ask an AI one question about it.
Write down its answer in one line.
For example: "Who won the last World Cup?"
Then verify that answer against two other sources (SP-3).
Tell the class if the sources agreed or not.
