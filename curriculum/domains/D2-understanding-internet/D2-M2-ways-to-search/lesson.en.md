# Ways to Search

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

This lesson serves **O03**.
By the end, you can **choose the right way to search**.
You can also switch between search modes and refine a search.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

A *search engine* helps you find things on the internet.
You type words, and it shows results.
There is more than one kind of result.
Picking the right kind saves your time.

There are six common ways to search.
Use **Web (All)** to find pages and written answers.
Use **Images** to find pictures of a thing.
Use **Videos** to see how to do something.
Use **Maps** to find places and the way there.
Use **News** to find recent stories.
Use **Shopping** to find prices of things to buy.

On a real search page, these appear as *tabs* near the top.
You will see **All, Images, Videos, Maps, News, Shopping**.
*All* is the normal view that mixes results.
A newer tab, **AI Mode**, writes an answer for you.
AI Mode can be wrong, so always verify it (SP-3).
You can tap a tab to switch the kind of results.

You can also search without typing.
Tap the **microphone** icon to speak your search (voice search).
Tap the **camera** icon (Google Lens) to search with a photo.
This helps when you cannot spell a word.

Sometimes the first results are not good.
You can *refine* your search to do better.
First way: add more words to be clear.
"Tomato" is broad, but "tomato farming Nepal" is clear.
Second way: use quotes for exact words.
"football rules" keeps those two words together.

If results are still bad, do not give up.
Recovery step one: use simpler, shorter terms.
Recovery step two: change one keyword for another word.

Stay safe while you search.
Turn on *safe-search* in the settings to hide unsafe results.
If a result asks for personal information or payment, close it.
Then try your search again (SP-1).

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

Here are the six ways to search.

| # | Way to search | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Web (All)** | Find pages and written answers. |
| 2 | **Images** | Find pictures of a thing. |
| 3 | **Videos** | Find videos that show how to do something. |
| 4 | **Maps** | Find places and the way to reach them. |
| 5 | **News** | Find recent news stories. |
| 6 | **Shopping** | Find prices of things to buy. |

> **Two helper words:**
> *Search engine* — a tool that finds things on the internet (खोज्न मद्दत गर्ने उपकरण).
> *Refine* — to improve your search words for better results (खोज शब्द सुधार्नु).

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this on a lab computer, with your facilitator near.

1. Open the browser and search one simple word, like **tomato**.
2. Look at the top of the results for the tabs (All, Images, Videos…).
3. Tap **Images**. See the results change to pictures.
4. Tap **Videos**, then **Maps**, then **News**. Watch each one change.
5. If you see **AI Mode**, try it once — then verify its answer (SP-3).
6. Go back to **All**. Now refine: search **tomato farming Nepal**.
7. Try the **microphone** to search by voice. Say one word.
8. Try the **camera (Lens)** to search using a photo.

**Success:** you searched one word, switched modes, and tried voice or photo search.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Pick one thing about it to find.
Search it once, then switch to a different mode to see it another way.
For example: search a player in **All**, then switch to **Images** and **Videos**.
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
