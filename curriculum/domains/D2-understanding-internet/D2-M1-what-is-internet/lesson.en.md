# What Is the Internet?

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

This lesson serves **O02**.
By the end, you can **explain what the internet is**.
You can also show how information travels to a website.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

The *internet* is a big network that connects devices everywhere.
It lets your device share information with other computers.
A *device* is a phone or computer you use.

Think about three friends in a village.
A farmer wants to check the weather for tomorrow.
A shopkeeper wants to check the price of rice.
A student wants to watch a farming video.
All three ask the internet, and answers come back.

The internet can help you do many things.
You can learn from videos and lessons.
You can message family and friends.
You can find a place on a map.
You can read news and check prices.
You can find health and farming tips.
You can use some government services online.

How does an answer reach you?
Your question leaves your device first.
It travels through *Wi-Fi* to reach the internet.
Wi-Fi is one road that joins your device to the internet.
The internet carries your question to a website.
A *website* is a place with pages of information.
The website sends the page back to your screen.

Wi-Fi and the internet are not the same thing.
The internet is the whole big network.
Wi-Fi is just one road to reach it.
You can also reach the internet with mobile data.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

Look at each number in the picture. Here is each step.

| # | Step | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Your device** | The computer or phone you use to ask. |
| 2 | **Wi-Fi / router** | One road that joins your device to the internet. |
| 3 | **The internet** | The big network that carries information everywhere. |
| 4 | **Website / server** | Another computer that stores the page you open. |

> **Two helper words:**
> *Internet* — the big network that connects devices to share information (जानकारी साटासाट गर्ने सञ्जाल).
> *Wi-Fi* — one way a device joins the internet without wires (तारविना जोडिने तरिका).

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near you.

1. Point to **your device** on the picture. Say its name.
2. Trace the path with your finger, from 1 to 4.
3. Say each step aloud as you point to it.
4. Tell which step is Wi-Fi and which is the internet.

**Success:** you traced all four steps and named each one.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Tell how your question travels when you look it up online.
Name **two steps** the question passes through.
For example: "My question leaves my **device**, goes through **Wi-Fi**, then reaches the website."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
