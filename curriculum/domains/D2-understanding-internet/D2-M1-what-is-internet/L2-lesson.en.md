# Using a Web Browser

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

This lesson serves **O02**.
By the end, you can **open a browser and reach a website**.
You can also name the main parts of a browser.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

A *browser* is an app you use to open websites.
On a lab computer, it may be called Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
You open the browser first, then you reach any website.

A browser has a few main parts.
The most important is the *address bar* at the top.
You type a website name or a search there.
For example, type a name and press Enter.

The *back button* takes you to the page you saw before.
The *reload* button loads the same page again.
A *tab* shows which page is open.
You can keep many tabs open at once.
The big space below is the *page area*.
The website's words and pictures show there.

Stay safe in the browser.
If a page suddenly asks for your password or money, stop.
Close that page and tell your facilitator (SP-1).
Look at the picture below and tap each part.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| # | Part | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tab** | Shows which page is open; you can open many. |
| 2 | **Back button** | Takes you to the page you saw before. |
| 3 | **Reload** | Loads the same page again. |
| 4 | **Address bar** | Type a website name or a search here. |
| 5 | **Search box** | Some pages have their own search box too. |
| 6 | **Page area** | The website's words and pictures show here. |

> **Helper word:**
> *Browser* — an app used to open websites (वेबसाइट खोल्ने एप).

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this on a lab computer, with your facilitator near.

1. Open the **browser** app.
2. Click the **address bar** and type a website name.
3. Press Enter and wait for the **page area** to show.
4. Press the **back button**, then **reload** the page.

**Success:** you opened a website and used back and reload.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Open the browser and reach one website about it.
Name **two browser parts** you used to get there.
For example: "I typed in the **address bar**, and the page showed in the **page area**."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
