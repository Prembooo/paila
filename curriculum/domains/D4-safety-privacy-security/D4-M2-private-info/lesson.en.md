# Private Information

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can tell **private information** from public.
You can name what to keep to yourself online.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

Some things about you are fine to share.
Some things are private and should be kept safe.

**Private information** is data that can be used to find or harm you.
For example: your home address, phone number, and passwords.

**Public information** is safe to share with many people.
For example: your favourite game or a photo of a flower.

Think of private information like the key to your house.
You do not hand your house key to a stranger.

Be careful with photos too.
A photo can show your school, your home, or your daily path.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| Keep private | Usually safe to share |
|---|---|
| Home address | Your first name |
| Phone number | A favourite song |
| Passwords and OTP | A drawing you made |
| School and daily route | A general hobby |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. Your facilitator reads out a piece of information.
2. Decide: is it private, or safe to share?
3. Say your answer aloud, and one reason.
4. Try four examples this way.

**Success:** you sorted four items into private or public.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Make two short lists about your topic: "safe to share" and "keep private".
For example: "Safe: my team's name. Private: my phone number."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
