# Your Digital Footprint

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can say what a **digital footprint** is.
You can name what you leave online when you use the internet.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

When you walk on wet mud, you leave footprints.
Online, you also leave a kind of footprint.

A **digital footprint** is the trail you leave on the internet.
Every post, photo, like, and search can stay online.

Some footprints you make on purpose, like a photo you post.
Some happen quietly, like the websites you visit.

![Your online actions leave a trail of traces.](figures/digital-footprint.svg)

Your footprint can stay for a long time.
Other people may see it later, even teachers or future employers.
So it is smart to think before you share.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| What you do | The footprint it leaves |
|---|---|
| Post a photo | The photo can be saved or shared by others. |
| Write a comment | Your words stay under the post. |
| Search something | The app may remember your search. |
| Like a video | It can show what you enjoy. |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. Name one thing you did online this week.
2. Say what footprint it may have left.
3. Ask: would I be happy if my family saw it?
4. Name one thing you would keep private instead.

**Success:** you named one footprint and one thing to keep private.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Imagine you post about your topic online.
Write one thing that would be safe to share, and one to keep private.
For example: "I can share a match score. I keep my home address private."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
