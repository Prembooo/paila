# Digital Money Safety

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can name safe habits for digital money.
You can protect your OTP, PIN, and QR payments.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

Many people now pay with apps like **eSewa** and **Khalti**.
You can send money, pay a shop, or scan a **QR code**.

This is fast and useful, but it needs care.
Your **PIN** and **OTP** are the keys to your money.

The most important rule is simple.
A bank or wallet **never** asks for your OTP or PIN.

![A bank or wallet never asks for your OTP or PIN.](figures/otp-safety.svg)

Before you scan a QR code to pay, check the name shown.
Make sure it matches the real shop or person.
To receive money you never scan a QR or enter your PIN — only the person paying does that.

If a payment feels wrong, stop — ask the shopkeeper or someone you trust.
It is fine to take your time with money.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| Safe habit | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Keep your PIN and OTP secret | They unlock your money. |
| Check the name before you pay | You pay the right person. |
| Lock your wallet app | A lost phone stays safe. |
| Never pay to "claim a prize" | Real prizes do not need payment. |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near. Use a pretend payment.

1. Pretend you scan a QR code to pay a shop.
2. Say the name you would check before paying.
3. A "message" asks for your OTP — say your reply.
4. Name one way to lock your wallet app.

**Success:** you checked the name and refused to share your OTP.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Imagine you buy something for your topic with a wallet app.
Write the two checks you would do before you pay.
For example: "I check the shop name. I keep my PIN secret."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
