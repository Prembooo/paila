# Scams & Fake Media

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can spot a common online **scam**.
You can name three calm steps to stay safe.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

A **scam** is a trick to take your money or secrets.
Scammers often rush you and make you feel afraid.

A common scam is a fake SMS or call.
It may look like it is from eSewa, Khalti, or your bank.
It asks for your **OTP** or **PIN**.

Remember this rule: a bank or wallet **never** asks for your OTP or PIN.
If someone asks, it is a scam — even if the message looks real.

Today, AI can copy a voice or face.
A call may sound like a family member, but be fake.
So a familiar voice alone is no longer proof it is really them.
A scammer may say a family member needs money now — call the person back yourself first.

When something feels urgent or scary, slow down.
Use these three calm steps.

![If it feels unsafe: stop, do not share, ask someone you trust.](figures/safe-response.svg)

To check a strange call, hang up and call back on the real number.
You can agree on a family "safe word" to confirm it is really them.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

| Warning sign | Calm action |
|---|---|
| "Send your OTP now!" | Never share it. Banks never ask. |
| A rush: "Do it fast or lose money." | Slow down. Real services give you time. |
| A voice asks for money urgently | Call back on the known number to check. |
| A prize you did not enter | Ignore it. If it is too good, it is fake. |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. Your facilitator reads out a message or call.
2. Decide: is it safe, or a scam?
3. If a scam, say the three calm steps aloud.
4. Try three examples this way.

**Success:** you spotted a scam and named the three steps.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Write a short fake "scam message" about your topic, then write the safe reply.
For example: "Win free match tickets — send your OTP!" Safe reply: "I will not share my OTP."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
