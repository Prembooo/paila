# Can We Always Trust AI?

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can explain that AI can be wrong.
You can use two ways to verify an AI answer.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

AI is a helpful tool for learning and ideas.
But AI is not always right.

Sometimes AI gives a wrong answer with full confidence.
It can sound sure, even when it is wrong.
This is called an **AI hallucination**.
In simple Nepali: AI ले बनावटी कुरा साँचो जसरी भन्नु।

AI guesses the most likely words to say.
It does not always know if they are true.

So we do not trust one AI answer alone.
We **verify** it. To verify means to check if it is true.

**Way one:** cross-check with two independent sources.
Look for the same fact in two trusted places.

**Way two:** ask a trusted person, like your facilitator.
They can help you confirm the answer.

![Check an AI answer against two independent sources.](figures/verify-two-sources.svg)

**The rule:** never rely on one AI answer.
If it cannot be confirmed, or sources disagree, treat it as unverified.
Do not share it as true. Ask a trusted person.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

Here are the two ways to verify an AI answer.

| Way | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Cross-check** | Find the fact in two independent sources. |
| **Ask a person** | Confirm with your facilitator or a trusted adult. |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. Ask an AI a simple question. Read the answer.
2. Find the same fact in one trusted source.
3. Find it in a second, independent source.
4. If sources disagree, treat the answer as unverified.

**Success:** you verified one AI answer with two sources.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Write one question about your topic to ask an AI.
Then write the two ways you would verify its answer.
For example: "I ask about a football rule, then check a coach and a sports book."
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
