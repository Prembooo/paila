# Learning With AI

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can use AI to help you learn.
You can ask good questions and still verify facts.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

AI can be a helpful study partner.
It can explain ideas in many simple ways.

Here are four ways to learn with AI.

**Ask a clear question.** Say exactly what you want to know.
For example: "What is a food chain in simple words?"

**Ask follow-ups.** If you want more, keep asking.
For example: "Can you give an example from my village?"

**Ask for a simple summary.** Ask it to make ideas short.
For example: "Sum this up in three short lines."

**Ask for a translation.** Ask for English or Nepali.
For example: "Say this in simple Nepali."

AI translations and summaries can also be wrong.
Read them with care, and ask your facilitator if unsure.
But always verify important facts.
Check them with two sources or a trusted person.

These skills build toward your final project in Domain 6.
There you will use AI to make something of your own.

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. Ask AI one clear question about a school topic.
2. Ask one follow-up to learn more.
3. Ask for a simple summary in three lines.
4. Verify one important fact with a second source.

**Success:** you asked, followed up, summarized, and verified one fact.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Write a clear question and one follow-up to ask AI about it.
Then write one fact you would verify with another source.
For example: "What is offside in football? Now give an example." I check it with a coach.
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
