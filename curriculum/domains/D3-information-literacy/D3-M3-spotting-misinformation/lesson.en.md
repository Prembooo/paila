# Spotting Misinformation

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can spot two warning signs of misinformation.
You can take a calm action before you share.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

Misinformation means news or facts that are wrong.
Sometimes people share it without knowing it is wrong.

You can learn to spot it with warning signs.
A warning sign is a clue to slow down and check.

**Sign one:** a big, shocking claim with no source.
It may say "amazing" or "shocking", but shows no proof.

**Sign two:** it asks you to share fast.
It may say "send to everyone now!"
Real news does not need you to rush.

**Sign three:** a photo or voice may be made by a computer (AI).
If it looks too perfect or strange, slow down and cross-check.

When you see a warning sign, do not worry.
Just take a calm action.

**Pause.** Do not share right away.
**Cross-check.** Look for the fact in two independent sources.
**Do not share** if it is still unverified.

![Cross-check a claim against two independent sources.](figures/verify-two-sources.svg)

This keeps you and your friends safe and calm.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

Here are the warning signs and the calm action.

| Warning sign | Calm action |
|---|---|
| Big shocking claim, no source | Pause and check two sources |
| "Share this fast!" | Do not rush; verify first |
| Two sources disagree | Treat it as unverified; do not share |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. Read a short message your facilitator shows.
2. Look for a warning sign in it.
3. Say the calm action: pause, then cross-check.
4. Decide: share, or do not share yet?

**Success:** you found a warning sign and chose a calm action.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Write a short fake-looking message about your topic.
Mark one warning sign in it, and write the calm action.
For example: "WIN A FREE BALL — SHARE NOW!" has no source, so I pause and check.
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
