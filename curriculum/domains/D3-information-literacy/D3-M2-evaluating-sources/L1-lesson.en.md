# Who and Why Made This?

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can ask three questions about a source.
You can ask **who** made it, **when**, and **why**.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

A source is where a piece of news or fact comes from.
It can be a website, a video, or a message.

Not every source is the same.
Some sources are careful and honest.
Some are not careful at all.

Before you trust a source, do a small check.
Start with three simple questions.

**Who made it?** A name or group you can find is better.
**When was it made?** Old news may now be wrong.
**Why was it made?** To help you learn, or to sell something?

![Check who, when, and why made a source.](figures/source-check.svg)

These three questions are the start of a trust check.
We will learn more questions in the next lesson.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

Here are the three questions and why they help.

| Question | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| **Who** made it? | A clear name is easier to trust. |
| **When** was it made? | Old facts may have changed. |
| **Why** was it made? | To help, or only to sell? |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. Look at one web page or video together.
2. Ask: **who** made it? Find the name.
3. Ask: **when** was it made? Find the date.
4. Ask: **why** was it made? Say your guess.

**Success:** you asked all three questions about one source.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Imagine you found a page about your topic.
Write the three questions you would ask about it.
For example: "Who made this farming page? When? Why — to help or to sell seeds?"
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
