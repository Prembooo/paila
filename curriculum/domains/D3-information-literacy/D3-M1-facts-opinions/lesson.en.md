# Facts and Opinions

## 1. Learning Goal (सिकाइ लक्ष्य)

By the end, you can tell a **fact** from an **opinion**.
You can use one simple test to check.

## 2. Let's Learn (सिकौं)

Every day we hear many things from people.
Some of these things are facts. Some are opinions.

A **fact** is something we can check.
We can find out if it is true or false.
For example: "Today it is raining." We can look outside.

An **opinion** is what someone feels or likes.
It is not true or false for everyone.
For example: "Rainy days are the best." Some agree, some do not.

Use one simple test: **"Can we check it?"**
If yes, it is likely a fact.
If it is only a feeling, it is an opinion.

![A fact can be checked; an opinion is a feeling.](figures/fact-opinion.svg)

Both facts and opinions are fine to share.
But it helps to know which one you are hearing.

## 3. Look and Name (हेरौं र नाम भनौं)

Here are three pairs from village life.

| Fact (we can check it) | Opinion (a feeling) |
|---|---|
| The bus leaves at 7 in the morning. | The morning bus is the best one. |
| This goat gave milk today. | Goat milk tastes better than buffalo milk. |
| The maize field is one bigha. | Our maize field is the prettiest one. |

## 4. Practice (अभ्यास)

Do this with your facilitator near.

1. Listen to a sentence your facilitator says.
2. Ask yourself: **"Can we check it?"**
3. If yes, say "fact". If no, say "opinion".
4. Try three more sentences the same way.

**Success:** you sorted four sentences into fact or opinion.

## 6. Activity (क्रियाकलाप)

Pick a topic you like — for example football, a song, farming, or your village. Any topic is fine.
Write one fact and one opinion about your topic.
For example: "Football has two teams" is a fact. "Football is the best game" is an opinion.
There is no single right answer — your choice is what matters.
