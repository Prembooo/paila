# Readability & Language Audit (पढाइ सहजता र भाषा अडिट)

> Purpose: sample lessons and confirm they are easy to read in Nepali, with short sentences,
> few English loanwords, and clear meaning. Run by a **native Nepali facilitator**.
> उद्देश्य: लेसनहरू नेपालीमा सजिलो छन् कि छैनन् जाँच्ने।

This audit supports authoring rules (sentences ≤ 15 words; ≤ 5 English loanwords per page in D1;
term-before-use) and complements the per-lesson `content-review-checklist.md`.

---

## 1. Sampling procedure (नमुना छनोट)

1. Pick **2 lessons per domain** (D1–D6) — 12 lessons total. Include at least one D1 lesson (strictest loanword rule).
2. From each chosen lesson, take **one full page** of the Nepali version (`lesson.ne.md`).
3. If the lesson is short, audit the whole lesson as one "page".
4. Record results in the scoring table below, one row per page.

## 2. What to measure per page (प्रति पृष्ठ नाप्ने कुरा)

- **Longest sentence (words):** count words in the longest Nepali sentence. Target **≤ 15**.
- **% sentences ≤ 15 words:** target **100%**.
- **English loanwords:** count distinct English-origin words written in Latin or as transliteration that have a common Nepali equivalent. Target **≤ 5 per page** (D1 strict).
- **New terms defined first:** every new term has its Nepali meaning **before** first use. Yes / No.
- **Clarity read-aloud:** read the page aloud to one learner-level listener. Did they understand on first hearing? Yes / Partly / No.

## 3. Reading level (पढाइ तह)

- Target level: **phase1-low-english** (front-matter `reading_level`).
- Confirm: a learner with basic literacy can read the Nepali page without help.
- Flag any sentence needing re-reading, or any word a typical villager would not know.
