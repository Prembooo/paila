# Safety & Tone Audit (सुरक्षा र भाव अडिट)

> Purpose: confirm the curriculum is **safety-first and never fear-based**. Every risk must be
> paired with a calm, constructive action, and the right safety pattern (SP-1…SP-5) must be referenced.
> उद्देश्य: हरेक जोखिमसँग शान्त, सकारात्मक कदम जोडिएको छ भनी पुष्टि गर्ने।

Run on **every lesson that mentions a risk** (scams, money, privacy, distressing content, AI errors).

---

## 1. Procedure (कार्यविधि)

1. Read the lesson and list every **risk or warning** it mentions (one row each in the table below).
2. For each risk, find the **paired action** — the calm step the learner should take.
3. Check that the matching **safety pattern ID** is referenced where expected (see map in §3).
4. Scan the whole lesson (English **and** Nepali) for **fear or shame language** (see §4 word list).
5. Confirm front-matter `safety_actions_paired: true`.

## 3. Safety pattern reference map (सुरक्षा ढाँचा नक्सा)

Reference the matching pattern by ID where the situation appears:

- **SP-1** — Suspected scam / unsafe situation → Stop · Don't share · Consult a trusted person. *(D4 scam lessons, any shown risk; supports O08)*
- **SP-2** — Unexpected money/credential request → Refuse · Verify via a trusted channel · Never share PIN/OTP/password. *(D4-M5 digital money; supports O11)*
- **SP-3** — Verify before you rely → Cross-check ≥ 2 independent sources; AI can be confidently wrong. *(D3 lessons, every AI verify step; supports O05, O06)*
- **SP-4** — Feeling upset/distressed online → It's okay to pause · Talk to a trusted adult/facilitator. *(D5 attention/emotion lessons; supports O10)*
- **SP-5** — Shared/family device hygiene → Don't save logins · Clear personal data before passing on. *(any Practical Activity on shared devices; supports O07)*

## 4. Fear / shame language scan (डर/लाज भाषा जाँच)

Flag and rewrite any wording that scares or shames the learner. Watch for:

- Threatening phrasing: "you will lose everything", "it's too late", "you are in danger".
- Blame/shame: "it's your fault", "only foolish people…", "you should be ashamed".
- Absolutes that induce panic: "never safe", "always hacked", "everyone is watching you".

**Rewrite pattern:** state the risk plainly → give the calm action → point to the SP step.
Example: instead of "Scammers will steal your money!" use "If a message asks for money unexpectedly, refuse and verify through a trusted channel (SP-2)."
