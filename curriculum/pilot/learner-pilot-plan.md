# Learner Pilot Plan (सिकारु पाइलट योजना)

> Purpose: run **one full domain** with a small learner cohort and confirm the domain's
> **outcomes (O01–O12) are demonstrably met**. Capture confusion points and loop findings back to authoring.
> उद्देश्य: सानो समूहसँग एउटा डोमेन चलाएर आउटकम पूरा भयो कि भनी पुष्टि गर्ने।

---

## 1. Setup (तयारी)

- **Cohort:** 5–8 learners matching the real target group (rural, basic literacy).
- **Domain:** pick one domain (D1–D6) and run **all its lessons** in order.
- **Outcomes in scope:** list the domain's outcomes from `architecture/outcomes.md`. Examples:
  - D1 → **O01**
  - D2 → **O02, O03**
  - D3 → **O04, O05, O06**
  - D4 → **O07, O08, O11**
  - D5 → **O09, O10**
  - D6 → **O12**
- **Facilitator:** use the same guide-only setup as the facilitator pilot where possible.

Watch especially for: words learners didn't understand, instructions they couldn't follow alone, AI verify steps skipped, offline alternative unclear, any risk that caused worry (check tone vs SP-1…SP-5).

## 4. Quick learner feedback (सिकारुको प्रतिक्रिया)

Ask the cohort, simply and supportively:
- What was easy today? के सजिलो थियो?
- What was hard or confusing? के गाह्रो वा अलमल भयो?
- What would help you next time? अर्को पटक के ले मद्दत गर्छ?

## 5. Feedback loop (सुधार चक्र)

1. Group confusion points by lesson; turn each into a concrete authoring edit.
2. Send edits back to authoring (language, examples, structure, safety tone).
3. Re-run the affected lessons or the readability/safety audits as needed.
4. Record which outcomes still fall below 80% and prioritize those lessons.
