# Phase 1 Outcomes (Phase_1_Outcomes)

These are the master-goal capabilities every learner should demonstrate by the end of Phase 1. Each has a stable ID, an English and Nepali statement, an **observable check** (how a facilitator confirms it), and the primary domains that build it.

> Tracing rule: every Learning_Module maps to ≥1 outcome here, and every outcome is served by ≥1 module. (Requirements 1.2, 1.3, 1.9, 2.1)

| ID | Statement (English) | कथन (नेपाली) | Observable check | Primary domains |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **O01** | Operate a smartphone/computer to complete a named basic task unassisted | सहयोगविना मोबाइल/कम्प्युटरमा तोकिएको आधारभूत काम गर्न सक्ने | Learner powers a device on/off and opens a named app without help | D1 |
| **O02** | Explain what the internet is and reach information through a browser or app | इन्टरनेट के हो भन्ने बुझ्ने र ब्राउजर/एपबाट जानकारीमा पुग्न सक्ने | Learner opens a browser and reaches a named site/app | D2 |
| **O03** | Search effectively across result types and refine search terms | विभिन्न प्रकारका नतिजामा खोज्न र खोज शब्द सुधार्न सक्ने | Learner finds a target answer and improves one weak search | D2 |
| **O04** | Distinguish facts from opinions and apply a source-trust checklist | तथ्य र विचार छुट्याउने र स्रोत भरपर्दो छ कि छैन जाँच्ने | Learner labels 3 items fact/opinion and runs the 4-question check | D3 |
| **O05** | Recognize misinformation and cross-check a claim against ≥2 independent sources | गलत सूचना चिन्ने र दाबीलाई कम्तीमा २ स्वतन्त्र स्रोतसँग मिलाउने | Learner flags a misleading claim and names 2 independent checks | D3 |
| **O06** | Use the AI companion and verify its answers before relying on them | AI सहयोगी प्रयोग गर्ने तर भरोसा गर्नुअघि जवाफ जाँच्ने | Learner asks the AI, then verifies the answer against 2 sources | D3 |
| **O07** | Protect personal information and recognize a digital footprint | व्यक्तिगत जानकारी जोगाउने र डिजिटल फुटप्रिन्ट चिन्ने | Learner names what to keep private and one footprint example | D4 |
| **O08** | Recognize common scams and synthetic media, and respond safely | सामान्य ठगी र नक्कली मिडिया चिन्ने र सुरक्षित तरिकाले प्रतिक्रिया दिने | Learner spots a scam's warning sign and states the safe response | D4 |
| **O09** | Use social media responsibly and build a positive digital identity | सामाजिक सञ्जाल जिम्मेवारीसाथ चलाउने र सकारात्मक डिजिटल पहिचान बनाउने | Learner picks the respectful response and one identity action | D5 |
| **O10** | Recognize attention/emotional manipulation and apply a coping strategy | ध्यान/भावना तान्ने चलाखी चिन्ने र सामना गर्ने उपाय लगाउने | Learner names a design trick and one coping step | D5 |
| **O11** | Use mobile money safely by refusing and verifying suspicious requests | शंकास्पद अनुरोध अस्वीकार गरी जाँचेर मोबाइल पैसा सुरक्षित प्रयोग गर्ने | Learner refuses a fake request and verifies via a trusted channel | D4 |
| **O12** | Plan and complete a self-directed learning project (capstone) | आफ्नै सिकाइ परियोजना योजना गरी पूरा गर्ने (क्यापस्टोन) | Learner produces a cited, verified capstone artifact | D6 |

## Notes
- O06, O10, O11 are reinforced by the cross-cutting threads (AI literacy, emotional/attention defense, digital money) — see `domain-map.md` thread matrix.
- Outcome wording is intentionally behavior-based so assessment is observable (Requirement 2.1).
