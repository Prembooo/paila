# Phase 1 "Digital Citizen" Curriculum — Quality & Accuracy Audit

**Scope:** All 31 lesson pairs (`lesson.en.md` + `lesson.ne.md`) across Domains D1–D6, their `facilitator.ne.md` guides, the D*-M9 review/quiz pages, plus the architecture and template files. Reviewed against the locked teach-first template, the outcome map (O01–O12), bilingual-parity rules, and the `renderQuiz` block format in `site/build.js`.

**Method:** Six parallel domain audits, each reading every file in its domain and web-verifying time-sensitive claims. Findings below are de-duplicated and merged. Where a claim was checked against the running build, that is noted. **No lesson files were modified in this pass** — this report is the only output.

**Date of audit:** 2026-06-29.

---

## 1. Summary

### Overall quality
The curriculum is in **good shape**. The locked structure is followed in all 26 content lessons; all 31 quiz blocks are **byte-identical between English and Nepali** (verified by direct comparison) and every marked answer index is **correct**; the calm, no-shame tone is consistent; and `क्रियाकलाप` (not `गतिविधि`) is used for "Activity" everywhere. There are **no Critical defects** and **no quiz-correctness errors**. The strongest concerns are (a) a small number of **currency gaps** in the safety domain (D4), (b) **terminology/title drift** between the architecture files and the shipped lessons, and (c) recurring **Nepali translation polish** — a few literal renderings, untranslated tech terms without a first-use gloss, and one outright meaning error.

### Top 5 themes
1. **Currency gaps in D4 (safety):** Lessons name the Nepal Cyber Bureau but give **no actual reporting channel**; the money lesson has **no "where to report" step at all**; password guidance still teaches an "8 characters" floor; and the dominant 2025 Nepal fraud patterns (QR "receive-money" scams, fake "payment received" SMS, AI voice-clone "family emergency" calls) are thin or missing. *(Highest-value fixes in the whole course.)*
2. **Title / terminology drift between architecture and lessons:** Several module titles in `domain-map.md` / `D*.md` don't match the shipped lesson titles (D3-M4, D5-M2, D5-M3, D6-M2), and core terms vary across files (cross-check = `दोहोर्‍याएर जाँच` vs `क्रस-चेक`; digital footprint = `पाइला` vs `डोब` vs `फुटप्रिन्ट`; `भरपर्दो` vs `भरपर्दा`).
3. **Nepali translation polish (priority):** Mostly natural, but with a real meaning error (`ढिलो शब्दले` for "loose words"), wrong word choices (`विश्वासी` for "confident"; `हाकिम प्रोग्राम` for OS; `बचत` for file storage), untranslated tech terms with no gloss on first use (`OTP/PIN/QR`, `किवर्ड`, `ह्यालुसिनेसन`, `अटोप्ले`, `नोटिफिकेसन`, `बायो`), and a few parity omissions (notably the safety phrase "trusted **adult**" losing "adult" in D5-M4, and the M5 activity example dropping a clause).
4. **AI-literacy depth:** The "verify AI answers" rule is present where required, but the *why* is rarely explained (AI predicts likely words; sounds confident while wrong; can invent fake sources), and verify is applied to "facts" but not to AI **translations/summaries** — the highest-risk AI use for weak-English learners.
5. **Figure/relevance gaps & engagement:** A few image/text mismatches (D2-M2 caption describes a search page the figure doesn't show), abstract concepts lacking a diagram (D6-M3 project planning; D2-M3 filter bubble), thin in-body local-rural anchors (the body teaching is generic even where Activities are localized), and D5 is phone-centric while learners are in a computer lab.

### Cross-cutting note that changes severity (verified against the build)
All 18 lessons that print `This lesson serves **O0X**.` / `यो पाठले **O0X** लाई सघाउँछ।` in the body, and the review pages that say `Domain`/`डोमेन`, **are cleaned automatically at render**: `sanitizeLearner()` (`site/build.js:431`) strips the "This lesson serves…" line, and the relabel at `build.js:444` turns `Domain→Chapter` / `डोमेन→अध्याय` before display. So these are **source-hygiene / authoring-rule** issues (the template forbids them in source), **not** learner-facing bugs. They are recorded at **Low** severity accordingly. *(Caveat: the `D*.md` domain-overview pages are not passed through `sanitizeLearner`, so any outcome codes printed there would show if those pages are ever published — verify whether `D*.md` is learner-facing.)*
